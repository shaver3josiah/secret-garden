Beaver, PA

The scene follows the real sky at your location. Tonight the moon is waxing. Day cycle plays the whole day, sunrise to stars, in a few minutes.

Live data comes from open sources that need no account: Open-Meteo for weather and air, RainViewer for rain radar, OpenStreetMap for maps, OSRM for road routes, and BigDataCloud for the towns along the way. The moon phase and compass work on the device, offline.

Scripture is the public-domain Berean Standard Bible, chosen to echo the mood of the garden and the river.
